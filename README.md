## Featured projects

### [Sales Brain for Cursor](https://github.com/ertra/sales-brain-for-cursor)

An AI-powered sales research and enablement workflow for Cursor. It helps teams generate structured company profiles, product positioning, buyer personas, competitive battlecards, objection handling, sales plays, and case study drafts from public company context.

Built for founders, sales leaders, enablement teams, and RevOps people who need to create useful GTM materials without starting from a blank page.
